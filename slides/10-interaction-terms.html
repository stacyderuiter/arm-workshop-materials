Interaction Terms

June 2025

Interaction Terms

  • Relationship between the response and predictor varies with the value of another predictor
  • Hierarchy principle: main effects needed when interaction effect is present
    • Main effect: the explanatory variable on its own
    • Interaction effect: the product of two explanatory variables

Case Study

Is there a relationship between economic growth and energy consumption?

  • Data from Goshrey et al. (2018)
  • Variables
    • year Calendar year
    • GDP US gross domestic product
    • EC Energy consumption in billion BTU
    • O_P Oil price in dollars
    • SPE Public spending in dollars

The Model

  • Ideally, control for spending and oil price
  • Energy consumption and oil price my interact to influence economic growth
  • Greater volatility in oil prices after 1972, may interact with energy consumption

Regression Model: Categorical Interaction Term

\[GDP_{i} = \beta_{0} + \beta_{1}EC_{i} + \beta_{2}V_{i} + \beta_{3}EC_{i}*V_{i} + \epsilon_{i}\] - \(V_{i}\) is an indicator variable
- Before 1972 has a different slope then after 1972

When \(V_{i} = 1\)

\[GDP_{i} = \beta_{0} + \beta_{1}EC_{i} + \beta_{2}V_{i} + \beta_{3}EC_{i}*1 + \epsilon_{i}\]

\[GDP_{i} = \beta_{0} + (\beta_{1}EC_{i}+\beta_{3}EC_{i}) + \beta_{2}V_{i} + \epsilon_{i}\]

\[GDP_{i} = \beta_{0} + (\beta_{1}+\beta_{3})EC_{i} + \beta_{2}V_{i} + \epsilon_{i}\]

When \(V_{i} = 0\)

\[GDP_{i} = \beta_{0} + \beta_{1}EC_{i} + \beta_{2}V_{i} + \beta_{3}EC_{i}*0 + \epsilon_{i}\]

\[GDP_{i} = \beta_{0} + \beta_{1}EC_{i} + \beta_{2}V_{i} + \epsilon_{i}\]
